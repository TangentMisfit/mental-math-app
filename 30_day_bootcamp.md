# 30-Day Mental Math Bootcamp

**Daily time:** 15–20 minutes
**Goal:** Fluent mental addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, and percentages

## WEEK 1 — Number Sense & Recall
- Day 1: Complements to 10/100; doubles/halves to 3 digits; app mode: add (beginner)
- Day 2: 1–12× tables; squares to 15²; app mode: mul (beginner)
- Day 3: Skip-count by 7s/9s; rounding to nearest 10/100; app: sub (beginner)
- Day 4: Fraction↔%: 1/2, 1/4, 1/5, 1/10, 1/8, 1/3; app: pct (beginner)
- Day 5: Mixed recall; app: mixed (beginner)
- Day 6: Weak-spot day (review your lowest accuracy mode)
- Day 7: Rest or light mixed (beginner)

## WEEK 2 — Fast Add/Sub
- Day 8: Left-to-right addition; app: add (intermediate)
- Day 9: Compensation; bridging tens/hundreds; app: sub (intermediate)
- Day 10: Multi-number sums (3–5 terms); app: mixed (add/sub focus)
- Day 11: Speed round (2×60s); app: add, sub (intermediate)
- Day 12: Word problems (differences, totals); app: mixed
- Day 13: Weak-spot day
- Day 14: Rest or light mixed

## WEEK 3 — Multiplication
- Day 15: Distributive property; app: mul (intermediate)
- Day 16: ×5, ×9, ×11 tricks; app: mul (intermediate)
- Day 17: Near-base × (close to 100); app: mul (intermediate)
- Day 18: Squares and close-products; app: mixed (mul-heavy)
- Day 19: Speed round (3×60s); app: mul
- Day 20: Weak-spot day
- Day 21: Rest or light mixed

## WEEK 4 — Division & Percentages
- Day 22: Chunking division; divisibility; app: div (intermediate)
- Day 23: Compatible numbers; halving/doubling for divisors; app: div
- Day 24: Base % (1,5,10,20,25,50,75); app: pct (intermediate)
- Day 25: Build-up % (e.g., 35%=25%+10%); app: pct
- Day 26: Reverse % (find original); app: pct
- Day 27: Mixed pressure test (4×60s); app: mixed
- Day 28: Weak-spot day
- Day 29: Capstone: real-life problems (shopping, unit rates, splits); app: mixed
- Day 30: Final test: 5×60s across all modes; review progress.csv
